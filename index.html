ClearRead

LLM answers, without the wall of text.

A dyslexia-first reading view that re-renders any LLM's answer as a plain-language summary plus short, spoken-aloud chunk cards.

The problem

LLMs answer in dense, uniform blocks of text. For roughly 1 in 10 people with dyslexia, a wall of text means visual crowding, losing your line, working-memory overload, and fatigue. So we’ll show it, not just say it:

What every chatbot shows

Photosynthesis is the biochemical process by which green plants, algae, and certain bacteria convert light energy, usually from the sun, into chemical energy stored in the bonds of glucose and other carbohydrates, and it occurs primarily within specialized organelles called chloroplasts that contain the pigment chlorophyll, which absorbs light most strongly in the blue and red portions of the visible spectrum while reflecting green, and the overall reaction can be summarized as carbon dioxide plus water, in the presence of light energy, yielding glucose and oxygen, a process divided into the light-dependent reactions that occur in the thylakoid membranes and produce ATP and NADPH, and the light-independent Calvin cycle reactions that occur in the stroma and fix carbon dioxide into sugar using that stored chemical energy.

The solution — built on evidence, not vibes

Every design choice traces to reading-science research or an accessibility standard.

  • Chunking into ≤50-word blocksW3C COGA
  • 66-character line lengthWCAG 1.4.8, British Dyslexia Association
  • Letter / word spacing 0.12 / 0.16emZorzi 2012 (PNAS), WCAG 1.4.12
  • Summary-first progressive disclosureW3C COGA
  • Per-chunk read-aloud, in any orderBimodal (see + hear) reading evidence
  • Cream background, never justifiedBDA Style Guide 2023

Dyslexia fonts? Evidence says spacing beats special fonts — so OpenDyslexic is an optional toggle, never the default.

Works with any LLM

ClearRead is a display layer, not a chatbot. Bring your own answer.
